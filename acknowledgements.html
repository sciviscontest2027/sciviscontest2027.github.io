Data

The Missing Struts dataset was created at Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory. Jenny Nicolino served as PI for the project. Alex Hamza originally suggested the idea, and Karl Fisher led the resonant ultrasound spectroscopy (RUS) effort, with David Stobbe performing the initial RUS work and Brian Tran later taking over analysis and paper writing. The lattice design and printing effort included work by Paul Alexander, Steve Burke, and Robert Spence, who cut the parts from the build plate.

This work was performed under the auspices of the U.S. Department of Energy by Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory under contract DE-AC52-07NA27344.

The contest is co-organized with IEEE VIS. Past contests are archived at the SciVis Contest site.
